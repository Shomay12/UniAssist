What it does

More than a chatbot.

The assistant is designed around your academic knowledge base. Retrieval happens before generation, allowing answers to be grounded in the material available to the system.

01

Ask questions

Ask natural-language questions about your uploaded lectures, notes, books, and academic documents.

02

Retrieval first

Relevant chunks are retrieved from the knowledge base and supplied to the LLM as contextual evidence.

03

Source-aware answers

Retrieved documents and page information can be displayed alongside the generated response.

04

Subject-based learning

Academic material can be organized by subjects such as FDSA, Cloud Computing, and Networking and Data Communication.

05

Local knowledge base

Your documents are transformed into embeddings and stored in a vector database for semantic retrieval.

06

Built for learning

Future capabilities include quizzes, flashcards, revision notes, and exam preparation.

Architecture

Retrieval → Context → Generation

Research foundation

Built around the RAG paradigm.

The project follows the core idea introduced in the foundational RAG research: combine a language model's parametric knowledge with an external non-parametric knowledge source retrieved at generation time.

Foundational RAG paper · NeurIPS 2020

Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks

Patrick Lewis, Ethan Perez, Aleksandra Piktus, Fabio Petroni, Vladimir Karpukhin, Naman Goyal, Heinrich Küttler, Mike Lewis, Wen-tau Yih, Tim Rocktäschel, Sebastian Riedel, Douwe Kiela.

The paper presents RAG models that combine pre-trained sequence-to-sequence generation with a dense vector index accessed through a neural retriever. It explores retrieval-augmented generation for knowledge-intensive tasks and demonstrates the value of retrieving explicit external knowledge during generation.
